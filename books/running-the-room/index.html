Who it's for

This is for you if —

  • Your 1:1s, standups, or planning meetings have quietly turned into status updates nobody needs
  • You inherited a meeting cadence from someone else and never redesigned it for your team
  • You want a repeatable structure for meetings, not a one-off "great meeting" checklist

Probably not for you if —

  • You're looking for general facilitation theory — this book is almost entirely specific formats and agendas
  • You already run a tight, well-loved meeting cadence with your team
  • You want help with the first-year fundamentals of managing people — The Engineering Ladder covers that, and comes with this book in the bundle
