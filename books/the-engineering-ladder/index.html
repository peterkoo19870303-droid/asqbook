Who it's for

This is for you if —

  • You're a senior engineer who just took over your first team, or you're being considered for the move
  • You're already managing people but mostly running on instinct, without a system you trust
  • You want a clear picture of which skills actually differ between "being technical" and "managing people"

Probably not for you if —

  • You're looking for big-picture leadership theory — this book is almost entirely specific scenarios and tactics
  • You've been managing for 5+ years and already have a working system for 1:1s and promotion reviews
  • You want help with meetings specifically — Running the Room covers that, and comes with this book in the bundle
